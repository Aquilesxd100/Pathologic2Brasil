Recipes

Welcome to our guide on Recipes!
In this guide, we will show you the different types of "potions" available in the game and how to create them. We'll start with the most basic ones, the Tinctures, then Homemade Antibiotics, and lastly, the mysterious Homemade "Painkillers"!

TIP: If you have any doubts about where to find herbs, check out our "Useful Map" which shows the spawn locations and more, just click HERE.

BEWARE: To create Tinctures firstly you will need to fix the "Alembic" inside the Haruspex's Lair (unlockable after day 2). This requires 1x Spring and 1x Metal Scrap.
For Homemade Antibiotics and Painkillers, you'll need another machine, called "The Brewery". This one will demand 1x Metal Scrap and 1x Toolkit to repair it. A great way to obtain the Toolkit is by helping your roommate, "Sticky", with his plan on day 4.

Tinctures

Tinctures are mainly used to diagnose patients. Using them will reveal symptoms matching the color of the used Tinctured, if there are any. Yellow may reveal symptoms in "Nerves" layer , orange "Blood", and white "Bones". Another important use of Tinctures is for boosting your own immunity, or the immunity of others who are in infected zones (reducing the chances of infection).

- There are two types of Tinctures, the regular ones and the more potent versions. Visually, the stronger version will have bubbles inside the flask and a "(+)" next to their name.

Ok, but what are the differences between those versions?

Potent Tinctures may reveal two symptoms instead of one when diagnosing patients. On the other hand, the increase in the patient's pain will be higher.

Potent Tinctures boost your immunity and others even more!

TIP: Potent Tinctures are the BEST item in the game to boost people's immunity. Keep that in mind when trying to prevent infection of people who are located in an infected zone.

Antibiotics

The main use of Homemade Antibiotics is to treat infected patients. When using the correct one (with the same color as the patient's infection) the infection bar of the patient will decrease, giving them a higher chance of survival the next morning. If you are infected and use an antibiotic, your infection bar will decrease, along with your health, which will take a hit based on its current value.

TIP: Homemade Antibiotics will not cure you, or kill, no matter how low your health is.

Making Antibiotics

Antibiotics are made of Tinctures and Infected Organs/Blood. Just like Painkillers they take some hours to be produced, the higher the potency of the Antibiotic, the more time it will take to be created.

The potency of the Antibiotic affects:

How much The Fund bar will be filled when an ordinary patient is treated correcly.

The chance of survival for a named character located in an infected zone when treated correctly.

TIP #1: The color/type of the Antibiotic created will always match the Tincture used in its creation.

TIP #2: The potency of the Antibiotic is affected by the type of organ used. The more difficult the infected organ is to collect, the stronger the Antibiotic will be. Infected brains are the best, followed by hearts, livers, kidneys, and lastly, blood.

Ingredients

Painkillers

The main use of Homemade Painkillers is to alleviate patients pain, or at least, that's what it seems at first. What most people don't know is that this drug can have various other effects depending on what it was made from! Basically, the effects are: decrease in hunger, exhaustion, thirst, increase in immunity, and even a boost in the rate of exhaustion regeneration while sleeping!

TIP: All Painkillers, when used by you, increase your health recovery while sleeping for the next 2 hours. This effect stacks.

Making Painkillers

The creation of Painkillers is made combining Tinctures with healthy organs or blood. Just like Antibiotics, they take a few hours to be produced.

TIP #1: The potency of the effect is defined by the color/type of the Tincture used. See the possible combinations below.

TIP #2: The effects of the Painkillers vary depending on the organ used. Check out the details below.
