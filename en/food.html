Hunger

Welcome to our guide about hunger. Here you can learn multiple ways to get food.
Some methods may seem obvious but the tips that come within can be valuable!

TIP: Never eat any kind of nut. Despite the fact those items can be consumable, they did not decrease much of your hunger and can have way more value trading with children.

Trading

The simplest way is trading, with children, adults, guards or special characters.

BEWARE: The townfolks showed below will not always be like in the pictures, its common for them to have different hair colors or details in their cloths.

TIP #1: To see more about trades with the townfolks, check out our trading simulator app HERE.

TIP #2: To make an efficient use of the trade system, always make a pre-planning of your whole day before it even begin. Take in consideration the streets, parks and trading points in general you can pass throw while going where you need to. Remember, time is precious here.

TIP #1: The only recurring item you will find to trade with guards is lockpicks, don't worry about the others.

Buying from Bad Grief

The Bad Grief, leader of the outlaws found in The Warehouses, sells lockpicks for a fair price (in money). Despite the fact he only has a few, it's worth it to visit him daily. Just keep in mind he reestocks every night at 12:00 AM.

TIP: If Bad Grief is not in his usual place, trade with the other men in there.

Killing Bandits

This method, different from the others, comes with deadly risks. Before anything else if you didn't see our guide about combat check it out HERE. There I explain everything about this subject. Almost every bandit that dies/surrenders will have a lockpick to loot, even the disarmed ones!

BEWARE: If you get help of any guard or townfolk remember this! In the moment that bandit dies your "buddy" will try to get all the loot for himself, so be faster!

There is two kinds of bandits in the game, the ones with a palid face that spawn in normal districts of the town (non-infected/non-destroyed) during the night (00:00 AM - 07:30 AM). And the other type that spawns during all day and night in destroyed districts.

TIP #1: Hunting bandits with pale face is easier because they don't usually stick together like the others in destroyed districts. Those can be quite dangerous, beware!

TIP #2: Try to pick fights with unarmed bandits since they offer lesser risk and the same rewards as armed ones.

The Fund

The fund is a really important source of food in the game. To receive it, you need to complete your daily task (available since day 4) at the hospital (Theatre). After that, the fund can be claimed the next day at the Town Hall after 12:00 AM.

Don't forget about the fund progress bar; the higher it is, the more rewards (food) you will get!

BEWARE: The availability of the fund along with the progress bar, can be seen at any given time in your inventory in the upper right corner.

Availability: In case this symbol is gray, it means you haven't yet completed your daily task and will not receive anything from the fund. If the symbol is white, the fund is guaranteed.

Progress Bar: The higher the brown bar, the more items you will receive. Check below for ways to increase it.

Increasing the Fund Bar

  • Diagnosing and treating infected people (those lying on the ground) inside the theatre or infected zones.
  • Healing sick people in those same areas.
  • Rescuing babies and handing them over to the Town Hall.

TIP #1: Increasing the immunity of named characters or healing them does not affect the Fund Bar.

TIP #2: The better the quality of the antibiotic used in sick people, the greater the increase in the Fund Bar will be.

TIP #3: In case of doubt about the crafting of antibiotics, tinctures, infusions or analgesics, check out our full guide on "Potion Brewing" HERE.

Buying

As you probably already know, you can buy food from grocery stores. But beware, after day 7 those places don't accept money anymore! At that moment it's important to remember there are two places where they always sell food for money.

But which places are those?

  • The bar called "Broken Heart" doesn't have much food, but it's better than nothing.
  • Some kind of illegal grocery store called "Shady Shop", after day 7 it becomes a mandatory stop for you every day.

TIP #1: The price of food changes every day, but there's an important detail you should know. The more space the food occupies in your inventory, the better its cost-benefit ratio. In other words, you will pay less for it and reduce your hunger bar more effectively. However, since your time is limited, it’s not viable to visit all the stores at once. Keep that in mind.

TIP #2: If you are in the final days, there is a place where you can buy extra "Food Coupons". This place is called "Bad Grief's Nest" and is located in the warehouses. The coupons are expensive, so only buy them if you have money to spare or in case of an emergency.

Special
Events

Certain events in the game offer food as a reward. However, most of them take place on specific days and require a whole chain of actions to receive their rewards.

- Check out bellow the events you can get food from, the days they take place and more.

Day
1

Kind
Children

This event take place on day 1. After starting the game, head out to Bad Grief's Nest and talk to him. Then, go to the Soul-and-a-Halves Fortress. There, talk to a redhead girl sitting on a box. During the conversation she will offer you food, accept everything.

Day
1

Play
about Hunger

This event takes place on day 1, right after starting out. While walking around the town you will find some dark figures standing near the streets. Talk to as many as you can. One of them will mention a play about hunger. Ask him for a toast, and that's it!

TIP #1: That same character will comment something about stealing other people's houses. When answering him, say that you respect other's property. Then, afterwards, show interest in the act of stealing, and he will give you a lockpick along with the toast.

TIP #2: Among those dark figures, there is another one that can help you with food. This one will talk about money and regardless of how you respond, he will give you some (which can be used to buy food).

Day
1-4

Lara the
Kindheart

This event work in two parts. The first one takes place in Lara's house and is available from day 1 until the morning of day 4. During that time, just visit Lara's house and take all the food inside those lockers in her living room (1st floor). The second part happens on day 4, starting at 07:30 AM and remains available until midnight. All you need to do is talk to Lara in the Theatre/Hospital, then visit her house again. There, take all the food from the same place as before.

BEWARE: It is not necessary to help Lara with the water barrel issue to get the food in the second part.

Day
5-6

The Sacrifice

This one take place on day 5. To start it, you need to talk to an Odong that spawns in "The Hindquarters" (exact locations bellow). He will be there from 07:30 AM on day 5 until 07:30 AM the next day. Speak with him and express your interest in helping with the ritual as the butcher. After that, to receive the food, pay a visit to the location near the cemetery after 05:00 PM and before 07:30 AM the next day. There, talk with the Odong for the last time and interact with the bull to receive 2x steaks!

TIP #1: To save time, speak with the Odong for the first time after 05:00 PM. That way in the end of the conversation, he will invite you to the site of the Ritual, accept, and you will be teleported there to complete the event all at once!

TIP #2: When the Odong offers you a blade, accept it. This way, you will receive the second-best autopsy tool of the game!
